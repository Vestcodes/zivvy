Welcome to Zivvy — business software that finally feels made for operators.

This is a live walkthrough of the Business plan: the highest tier, with manufacturing, quality, and multi-company controls unlocked.

We start on the dashboard. One workspace for sales, stock, finance, people, and production — without hopping between tools.

Next, CRM. Leads and opportunities stay connected to the same customer record your sales team will invoice later. No duplicate data. No “which spreadsheet is true.”

From CRM we move into sales. Customers, quotations, sales orders, and invoices share one flow. When a deal closes, fulfillment and billing already know the context.

Inventory is next. Items and warehouses stay reconciled in real time, so purchasing and production are working from the same numbers.

Finance sits beside operations — payments and reports without a separate accounting silo. Your books reflect what the business actually shipped and billed.

HR and payroll are seat-aware and multi-tenant. Employees, attendance, and pay runs live next to the work they support.

Projects keep delivery visible for services and internal initiatives — tasks and ownership without another project tool.

On Business, manufacturing unlocks. Bills of materials and work orders connect shop-floor execution back to stock and costing.

Quality inspections close the loop — catch issues before they become returns or rework.

Finally, billing and team settings. Upgrade seats, manage plan, and keep access clean as you grow.
